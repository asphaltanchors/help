---
title: Can Asphalt Anchors be used without adhesive?
description: Why Asphalt Anchors must be installed with adhesive, and how the adhesive bonds the anchor to the asphalt.
sidebar:
  label: Is Adhesive Required?
---
No.

An anchor's strength comes from the adhesive bonding the anchor to the asphalt. Unlike concrete, asphalt needs both an anchor designed for it and the right adhesive.

Our anchors install into an oversize hole, where the anchor drops in cleanly. The adhesive fills the gap and bonds the anchor to the asphalt. See [EPX2 vs EPX3 Adhesive](/product-info/adhesive-options/) to choose one.
