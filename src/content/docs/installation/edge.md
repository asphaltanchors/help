---
title: Edge Installation
description: Minimum distance between an Asphalt Anchor and the edge of the asphalt.
sidebar:
  label: Edge Distance
---

Install our anchors at least 9 inches away from the edge of the asphalt area. This applies to every anchor model. As one gets closer to the edge, the asphalt may not be as consistent, and the area over which the forces are distributed starts shrinking. Installing closer than 9 inches will lead to premature failure.
