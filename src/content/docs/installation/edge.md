---
title: Edge Installation
description: Minimum distance between an Asphalt Anchor and the edge of the asphalt.
---

Install our anchors at least 9 inches away from the end of the asphalt area. As one gets closer to the edge, the asphalt may not be as consistent, and the area over which the forces are distributed starts shrinking. Installing closer than 9 inches will lead to premature failure.
